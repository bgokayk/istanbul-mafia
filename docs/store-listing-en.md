## Full description

The city sleeps. Unfinished business does not.

Istanbul Mafia: Night Shift is a survival action game with retro pixel art. Stay ahead of approaching enemies, position your character to make the most of automatic attacks, and shape each run through your level-up choices.

Start with KURT's control, USTURA's speed, ÇELİK's flames or ZARCI's talismans. Work toward unlocking characters such as AMCA and ŞOFÖR. Prepare for dangerous enemies such as TESTERE.

• Explore locations inspired by Istanbul, from the Grand Bazaar and Üsküdar waterfront to the squares of Beşiktaş and Taksim. Some areas require progression.
• Upgrade weapons, discover evolution combinations and equip extra powers.
• Complete quests and achievements to earn in-game gold and buy permanent upgrades.
• Time your dash and character abilities to find a way through the crowd.
• Play short runs with portrait touch controls, then try a different build on your next attempt.

The characters and story are fictional. The game includes stylized combat and weapon use.
